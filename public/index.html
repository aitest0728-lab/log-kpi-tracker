Full Perspective Forecast

Weighted Total Order forecast (HKTV Staff + ODS/VAN, from Tableau) minus estimated ODS orders = HKTV Orders ÷ your FT/PT Driver & Courier headcount plan → productivity — checked against both the Target Productivity and Target Cost/Order for each district

Per-District Staff Plan (Target Productivity blank = target from Full_Cost_Planning.xlsx · headcounts blank = 0)

For each district, searches FT/PT Driver and FT/PT Courier combinations (Driver/Courier split follows that district's latest Manpower Distribution ratio) across a reasonable staffing range — from the productivity ceiling (Target Productivity, using each district's manual override above if set) down to whatever the Overdrive % above still allows — and applies the cheapest combination whose achieved Productivity and Cost/Order both clear target. The search is also capped by the district's Staff Allocation Cap — the current ACTIVE FT/PT Driver/Courier headcount from the latest Logistics_Staff_List file — so a suggestion never proposes more heads in a bucket than are actually on the bench today; a plan that had to be trimmed this way is marked "bench-capped" on its badge. A district is flagged instead of filled in when no staffing level can clear both — genuinely possible when the forecast order volume is too low to amortize Fixed + ODS cost under the Target Cost/Order, too low relative to the Target Productivity for even a single staff member to clear it, or when the current bench itself is too small to reach the Cost/Order target even fully staffed.
